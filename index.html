A WITCH’S POCKET GUIDE

Follow the candy trail.

Bring down three towers

Remove an uncovered card whose rank is one above or below the waste card. Suits do not matter, and A wraps to K: K–A–2 is legal. Cards under other cards cannot be removed. Clear all 28 tableau cards to win.

Draw when you need a new path

Draw a fresh card from the 23-card stock whenever you choose. Drawing breaks your chain. There is no redeal. If the stock is empty, no card fits and you have no moon bridge, the deal is over.

Five treats make a little magic

Five removals in a row without drawing earn a moon bridge. Tap the bridge, then any uncovered card to remove it, regardless of rank. It breaks your chain. Hold one bridge at a time and earn at most two per deal. Undo restores the complete prior state.

All eight Journey deals have a verified route requiring no stock draws. Classic and Daily are shuffled challenges and may be unsolvable. The Daily deal changes at midnight UTC. Spot a move finds a legal move, not a complete solution.

Keyboard: Tab and Enter to select cards, Space to draw, Z to undo. Sound starts only after you switch it on. Best results stay on this device.
